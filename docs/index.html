Overview

This workshop brings together classroom educators and cybersecurity clinic practitioners for two days.

Day 1 stays in the classroom. It works through what large language models actually are and aren’t, and the ethics and academic-integrity questions that follow. From there it turns practical: lesson planning, feedback, differentiation, and accessibility. The day closes with what participants still need.

Day 2 turns to cybersecurity clinics: the experiential-learning model behind them, how AI is being integrated into that model, and how three states are running clinics of their own. The afternoon is a hands-on tabletop exercise built on Clinic-in-a-Box.

Materials are posted here as presenters supply them, and they stay up after the workshop.
